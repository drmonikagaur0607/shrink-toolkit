// qr-tool / 04

Generate a QR code.

Enter a URL, text, phone number, email address, or any other information and create a downloadable QR code.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is ever uploaded
Your QR code will appear here.

Privacy: the QR code is generated in your browser. Your text is not sent to a server.

Tip: for best scanning results, keep the QR code clear and avoid very long text when possible.
